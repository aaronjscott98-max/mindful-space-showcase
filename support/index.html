Find mental health support

Mindful Space shares information and self-care tools. It does not offer a crisis response, counselling, or diagnosis. If you need support, these services can help.

If someone is in immediate danger

Call 999 or go to your nearest A&E department.

Talk to someone

NHS 24 Mental Health Hub (Scotland)

Call 111 and choose the mental health option. Available 24/7 for urgent mental health help.

NHS urgent mental health information

Samaritans (UK and Ireland)

Free listening support: 116 123, available 24 hours a day.

Contact Samaritans

Breathing Space (Scotland)

Free listening support for people aged 16+. Call 0800 83 85 87. Monday–Thursday 6pm–2am; Friday 6pm–Monday 6am.

Breathing Space website

For everyday support

Speak with your GP, student support or counselling service, or a trusted person. Your university website may list confidential student wellbeing contacts.

Information applies primarily to Scotland; if you live elsewhere, follow your local health service guidance.
