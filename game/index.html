Ready to play?

Expect longer jumps, narrow ledges and fewer thought bubbles. Platform widths, gaps and heights change on every run. Can you finish all three levels?

How to play

Desktop: ← → or A / D to move; Space, ↑ or W to jump; Esc to pause.

Phone or tablet: hold ◀ or ▶ to move; tap Jump to jump; use Pause to take a break.

Encouraging thought bubbles are scarce: search several platforms between pickups and jump to reach higher ones. Some rare bonus bubbles appear on raised platforms. Flags mark checkpoints every five main platforms. Avoid challenging thoughts: they cost one point. Jumps get much harder in later levels. Falling returns you to your last checkpoint and costs one thought, if you have any.
