Accessibility at Mindful Space

Using the site

Pages have keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, a skip link, and responsive layouts. The Student Life game includes keyboard and touch controls, a pause button, and written instructions.

The game uses moving graphics and a canvas, which may not be suitable for everyone. The information and support pages do not require playing the game.

We have tested common interaction paths, but cannot guarantee accessibility on every device or with every assistive technology.

Found an accessibility problem?

This is a student project. If you are reviewing it, please share the affected page, your device/browser, and the problem with the project owner through the channel by which the site was shared.
